Research

Higher category theory, Homotopy Type Theory, and formalized mathematics in Lean. For the Lean code itself, see Formalization.

Papers

  • 2026
    A Certifying Proof Assistant for Synthetic Mathematics in Lean
    W. Nawrocki, J. Hua, M. Carneiro, Y. Xu, S. Woolfson, S. Rong, S. Hazratpour, S. Awodey · Proceedings of the 15th ACM SIGPLAN International Conference on Certified Programs and Proofs (CPP 2026), pp. 88–103

    Presents SynthLean, which embeds Martin-Löf type theory in Lean so that one can work both inside the type theory and in a model of it. A certifying normalization-by-evaluation typechecker proves automatically that internal definitions have sound interpretations in any model.

  • 2023
    Extremal problems of double stars
    E. Győri, R. Wang, S. Woolfson · Discrete Mathematics & Theoretical Computer Science 24(2)

    Counts how many copies of a double star a triangle-free graph can contain, and studies the reverse problem: the maximum number of edges or triangles in a graph under degree-sum conditions on its vertices.
