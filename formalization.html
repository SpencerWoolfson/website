Projects

  • 2024–now
    HoTTLean
    With S. Awodey, M. Carneiro, S. Hazratpour, J. Hua, W. Nawrocki, Y. Xu, and others

    A Lean formalization of the groupoid model of homotopy type theory, and of category-theoretic models of Martin-Löf type theories in general. The model interprets HoTT0, a fragment of HoTT in which univalence holds for sets. I contributed to the construction of a natural model with a univalent universe.

    GitHubReservoir
  • 2026
    SynthLean
    Part of HoTTLean

    A proof mode for doing mathematics synthetically inside a deeply embedded type theory, with a certifying typechecker that proves every internal definition is soundly interpreted in any model.
